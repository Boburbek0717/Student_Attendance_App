# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary visitors are universities and employers reading the public site as Boburbek Noraliyev's CV: they want to see his own scores, his teaching track record and the software he built. The course portal (attendance, practice, assignments) serves his own students and is secondary for now.

## Product Purpose
SAT Factory is the owner's SAT course and its course platform. The public homepage and results page work as a portfolio: they show who he is (SAT 1430, IELTS 8.5, student at the University of Szeged, teaching SAT online since 2024) and the results of students he taught. Success is a reviewer understanding, within one visit, that he scores highly, teaches students to high scores and built the platform himself.

## Positioning
A tutor whose own results and whose students' results are on the page as real numbers, one of whom (Allonazarov Javohir, 1470) scored above the tutor, and who wrote the platform the students use.

## Operating Context
The public pages are published as static files on GitHub Pages (https://boburbek0717.github.io/Student_Attendance_App/) from the `gh-pages` branch, generated by `scripts/export_static.py`. Links into the app (free practice, course portal) go to the Render portfolio demo, which sleeps when idle. Lessons are online, from Szeged to students in Uzbekistan; times display in Asia/Tashkent.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite and Jinja; server-rendered pages with small plain-JavaScript enhancements.
- Content Security Policy is `default-src 'self'`: fonts, images and scripts are self-hosted, and inline style attributes and inline scripts are not allowed.
- Public pages must render from an empty database (the static export uses one).
- Attendance and the Question Bank exist and must keep working, but are not current priorities.
- Undecided: the owner's public contact handle (`TUTOR['contact']` in app/portfolio_content.py is None, so contact buttons stay hidden).

## Brand Commitments
Name SAT Factory with the SF monogram logo (app/static/branding/sat-factory-logo.png). The owner's name is shown as Boburbek Noraliyev.

## Evidence on Hand
- Nine student results with Reading and Writing and Math scores in app/result_content.py; each has a short tag, three have the student's own written quote and six have a one-line summary. The owner confirmed the students agreed to be shown publicly with full names.
- The owner's scores: SAT 1430, IELTS 8.5.
- Real screenshots of the platform (fictional demo data) in app/static/portfolio/.
- No photo of the owner, no university programme or year, no press, reviews or other testimonials. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Real numbers over adjectives: every claim on a public page is a score, a fact or a quote that exists in the content files.
- The owner's own record and his students' results are told apart explicitly.
- Student quotes are secondary detail, available on demand rather than leading the page.
- Public pages stay static-exportable and fast; nothing on them depends on the course database.
